# Projects

**[ImageScript](https://github.com/imagescript/ImageScript)**
An image library for JavaScript and TypeScript with no dependencies at all. Every codec (PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF, JPEG XL, GIF and more) is plain TypeScript, so it runs on Node, Deno, Bun and in the browser without native add-ons or WebAssembly. It also renders SVG and text, including right-to-left scripts. Started in 2020, now downloaded about 800k times a month.

**[fluidd](https://github.com/fluidd-core/fluidd)**
The web interface for Klipper, the firmware behind many 3D printers. Contributions as a member of fluidd-core.

**coinop**
A cabinet-style arcade machine built around a Raspberry Pi with a touchscreen, physical buttons, a coin slot and a note acceptor. Comes with a custom touchscreen driver, a small custom server and its own games.

**bichael**
An Android app that unlocks an electric motorcycle when the rider walks up to it, but only once it is sure the phone is actually at the bike. Trips are reported to Home Assistant.

**bonbot**
A Discord bot that turns messages into images and prints them on a receipt printer, rendered with ImageScript.

**powerd**
A small daemon that keeps the machines of a Proxmox cluster switched off until something needs them, wakes them on demand and puts them back to sleep when they go idle. Works for hosts, VMs and containers.

**homelab**
Five Proxmox nodes with high availability, a self-managed firewall and router, Home Assistant, and monitoring that pushes alerts to a phone. The testbed for most of the other projects on this page.
